Kinematic and Control Analysis of Autonomous Ground Vehicles


This paper studies the kinematics and PID-based velocity control of autonomous ground vehicles (AGVs). Simulation and experimental results verify accurate trajectory tracking.

Autonomous ground vehicle, kinematics, PID control, trajectory tracking, motion planning.

Autonomous ground vehicles (AGVs) are widely used in logistics, agriculture, and surveillance. Accurate kinematic modeling is essential for precise motion control~\cite{siciliano}. Differential drive AGVs offer compact design and high maneuverability, but may experience wheel slip and stability issues at high speeds.
